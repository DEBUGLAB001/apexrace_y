PIT WALL REGULATIONS

  • Stops are chronologically organized by planned lap number.
  • Multiple different cars may schedule stops on the same lap (e.g. safety car pit window).
  • A single car cannot schedule two distinct stops on the identical lap.
  • Reordering with ▲ Earlier and ▼ Later adjusts the node's position in the linked list.

STEWARD CONSOLE

PENALTIES & REVERSIBLE ACTION STACK

REVERSIBLE STEWARD ACTIONS

Every decision is recorded on an undo stack. Undoing an action restores the full driver penalty state in the C++ backend and updates the live timing tower.

APPLY STEWARD ACTION

SANCTION POLICY

  • Time Penalties: Added to the driver's elapsed race time, affecting gap to leader and leaderboard position.
  • Warnings: Recorded on the driver's profile; 3 warnings trigger an automated penalty review.
  • Undo Stack: Fully restores the previous state, including penalty seconds and warning counters.
